# Voice and genre

Natural prose is situated. Use the user's destination and any real writing sample before adopting a default voice. A difference in genre may justify a different sentence, level of detail, or degree of personal presence.

| Destination | Useful emphasis | Protect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| School or academic report | Question, method, measured results, and the limit of each inference | Data, units, citations, uncertainty, rubric terms, and the requested person and formality |
| Technical explanation | The user's actual point of confusion and a concrete sequence or example | Exact behavior, prerequisites, commands, conditions, and failure modes |
| Business report | Decision, evidence, responsible actor, and trade-off | Baselines, commitments, dates, and material risks |
| Email or message | Reason for writing and requested action | Relationship, politeness, deadline, and boundaries |
| Article or essay | Coherent viewpoint supported by selected evidence | Attribution, argument scope, and meaningful complexity |
| Personal statement | Supplied choices and experiences in the applicant's own words | Actual biography, motives, and the difference between fact and reflection |
| Fiction or script | Character, scene, pace, and chosen artifice | Point of view, plot facts, dialogue voice, and intentional ambiguity |

## When a real voice sample is available

Read two or more paragraphs if available. Note a few reusable traits: degree of directness, typical sentence movement, favored level of detail, vocabulary, punctuation, and emotional distance. Compare the draft with those traits after revision. Do not copy phrases for their own sake, and do not transfer events, beliefs, or claims from the sample into the new text. If samples disagree or come from another genre, use only traits that fit the current task.

When no trustworthy sample exists, use a restrained voice suited to the task. Avoid inventing first-person experience, deliberate spelling mistakes, forced slang, or random rough edges. A formal voice can sound natural because it selects useful evidence and gives the reader a clear path through the reasoning.

## Depth of intervention

Light editing can repair a sentence. A substantial rewrite may need to move a result ahead of background, merge two repetitive paragraphs, or split one overloaded paragraph. Keep the source's meaningful order and required headings when they serve the assignment. A request to paraphrase every section does not authorize changes to data, references, quotations, or the strength of a conclusion.
